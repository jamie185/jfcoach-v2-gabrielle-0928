Onboarding asks
the right things.

The questionnaire is easier to move around, and it now asks what a trainer would ask before building anything.

  • The three lines are now a labelled Sections button. Tap any section to jump straight to it
  • Health check before any program: pregnancy or a baby in the last year, recent surgery, chest pain or dizziness, blood pressure that isn't under control, and diagnosed conditions. Any of these asks for GP, physio or senior trainer sign-off and tells the trainer
  • Training age is years of training plus a quick check (could you do 10 push-ups from the floor?). The lower of the two wins
  • Everyone is asked about joint pain and whether they're happy on the floor. Nothing is guessed from body size

Health Score and points
for the small things.

What to do next now fits the person, and meals, mood and breathing all count.

  • What to do next gives three different, relevant suggestions based on the client's goal category (Trim, Tone, Strong, Fit or Wellbeing). No more three versions of 'do a workout'
  • Challengers earn JF points: 5 for each meal logged (up to 4 a day) and 15 more for logging breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • Rating your mood earns 5 points a day, a breathing exercise 10, and hitting your chosen mood goal for the week 30
  • Every client's Health Score counts full meal days, the mood goal and breathing too, with honest 'what this adds' previews

Train together,
not just side by side.

One club chat for everyone with the same trainer, and a workout can go straight to one mate.

  • Community has a Your club chat row: challengers and Kickstart clients training with the same trainer, together
  • Challengers who only have JF support are routed into their club trainer's chat, so nobody is left out
  • Send a workout to one mate from your chat with them. It arrives as a card with Add to my calendar, and only the two of you can see it
  • When your mate adds it to a day, you both earn 15 Community points
  • Also fixed on the way: the share list lost its stray bullet points, and card headings keep their spacing when Community is opened directly

Wellbeing that
rewards the habit.

Breathing is a habit you can pick, and a mood goal gives the week a target.

  • Breathing exercise is one of the Wellbeing habits. A breathing session of a minute or more ticks it for the day
  • Mood goal: 3, 5 or 7 days a week, with progress for this week and the points it earns

Programs built the way
Gabrielle builds them.

The program builder and JFI now follow her training principles, with far more home, hotel, band and TRX options.

  • 96 new exercises (237 in the builder): TRX 13 beginner, 13 intermediate, 9 advanced; bands 33; bodyweight 56 for home, hotel rooms and outdoors
  • Every exercise has a training age. Diamond push-ups, Nordic curls and pull-ups are advanced only, never in beginner or intermediate programs
  • Regressions for older, pregnant and heavier clients: wall push-up, kneeling push-up, box squat, sit to stand, supported split squats with 'hold something steady'
  • Swap to something above your level and the app flags it and offers the easier option (e.g. Swap to Kneeling Push Up). JFI says the same when asked for a swap
  • JFI keeps the week balanced: a swap stays in the same movement pattern, and 'swap it for something else' now works instead of failing
  • Session length sets the count: 30 min is 4 to 5 exercises, 45 is 5 to 6, 60 is 6 to 8. Short sessions for intermediate and advanced clients end with one antagonist superset
  • Lower days alternate squat-led and hinge-led, pull days carry no chest work, push days are chest, triceps and side delts
  • Other activities are flagged by severity with a fix: netball on Saturday keeps leg days off Friday and Saturday
  • Next phase looks back: keeps the main lift that progressed, swaps skipped or painful accessories, adds a set when earned
  • Fat-loss goals get the 150 to 300 minutes of cardio a week aim, at a pace where you can still talk. Joint pain keeps jumping moves out

JFI's voice keeps up
and knows when to stop.

Quicker to speak and answer, stops the moment you talk, and ignores the room.

  • Speaks at a brisk, natural pace and answers first, in short turns
  • Stops the moment you talk over it: the phone silences JFI itself as soon as you say two words or 'stop', 'wait', 'hang on'. A quiet 'mm' or 'yeah' doesn't cut it off
  • Less background pickup: voice isolation on the microphone where the phone supports it, and JFI ignores music, TV, gym noise and other people's conversations
  • Same rules in the program builder and meal guide voice
  • Live now: the pace, interruption and background-noise rules. With the 1 October app update: the phone-side instant stop and voice isolation

Why not a speed dial?

OpenAI's GPT Live has no speaking-speed, turn-taking or noise setting (checked against their current SDK). So the speed comes from how JFI is told to speak, and the instant stop comes from the phone.

Gabrielle's training
principles, read through.

She asked whether we agree with them. Short answer: yes, with three small tweaks and two questions.

Agree

Nearly all of it, and it is now how the builder and JFI work. Screening first, training age that is checked not just claimed, one pull on every full body day, two exercises per muscle a week with 48 hours between, antagonist supersets only where they save time, and never inferring anything from body size.

Three small tweaks

  1. Add a lower-body check next to the 10 push-ups (for example 10 controlled box squats), so training age isn't judged on upper body alone.
  2. Let a client choose an advanced move after the heads-up rather than blocking it. That's how it works now.
  3. Deload when fewer than half the planned sessions were done in a phase, as well as on a schedule.

Two questions for Gabrielle

  1. Pregnancy and postnatal: should the program wait until the trainer ticks "cleared" in the app, instead of building with the pregnancy rules and flagging it?
  2. Older adults: the rules switch on at 60. Is that the right age, and should onboarding ask it directly?
